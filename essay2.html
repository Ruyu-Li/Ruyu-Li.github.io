Statement of Purpose

15 Mar 2026

I am currently a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa, advised by Prof. Yifan Wang. My research interests lie at the intersection of natural language processing and data management systems — particularly in how large language models can be made more efficient for long-context inference and how AI-driven approaches can transform traditional database query optimization.

My research journey began at Xinjiang University, where I earned my bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering. I then pursued my M.S. in Computer Science at the University of Nottingham under the supervision of Prof. Juan P. Garrahan, where I explored machine learning for astrophysics. That experience cultivated a strong foundation in applying data-driven methods to complex scientific problems and gave me a deep appreciation for interdisciplinary research. I later joined Westlake University, where I published an LLM-based recommender system paper at CIKM and gained hands-on experience in multimodal system development and AI for Science research.

Interests in Research

My current research interests center on two connected themes: efficient inference for large language models and AI-driven database systems. As LLMs are increasingly deployed in real-world applications, I am drawn to the challenge of making long-context inference practical — particularly through KV-cache optimization, where I see significant room for more intelligent memory management strategies.

I am equally interested in how LLMs can reshape traditional database systems. Much of query optimization today still relies on hand-crafted heuristics that break down with complex, multi-modal workloads. My recent work on Meta Engine explores this direction by unifying heterogeneous LLM-based query systems into a cohesive semantic query layer, and I am excited about extending this idea further. My earlier work on LLM-based recommender systems, published at CIKM 2025, also reinforced my conviction that the intersection of language understanding and structured data systems is a rich and underexplored space.

Long-Term Goals

My long-term goal is to pursue a faculty position in computer science, where I can build a research group focused on the synergy between large language models and data management systems. I believe that as LLMs become integral components of data infrastructure, there is a growing need for researchers who understand both the modeling and the systems perspectives. My diverse background — from astrophysics to recommender systems to database engines — has equipped me with the breadth to tackle problems at this interface, and I look forward to deepening this line of research throughout the remainder of my Ph.D. and beyond.
