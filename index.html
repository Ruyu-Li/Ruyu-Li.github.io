👋 About Me

Hi, I am Ruyu Li, a second-year PhD student at the University of Hawaiʻi, under the supervision of Prof. Yifan Wang. I previously completed my MSc at the University of Nottingham under the supervision of Prof. Juan P. Garrahan, and received my bachelor’s degree from Xinjiang University.

My current research focuses on LLMs, with ongoing work on KV-cache optimization and AI for databases. Previously, I conducted research on recommender systems, and during my master’s studies, I worked on machine learning for astrophysics.
